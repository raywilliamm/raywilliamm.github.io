About

I'm a Threat Intelligence analyst at Absega (ITG Holding), handling CTI reporting, IOC enrichment, threat actor profiling, and MITRE ATT&CK TTP mapping for banking,Insurance and customers in other sectors across Lebanon triaging alerts, tracking adversary behavior, turning raw indicators into reports people actually act on.

I work at the intersection of AI and security, not just as a user of AI tools, but hands on with the infrastructure behind them. I run local LLMs (Ollama + Qwen) on a VM, building a pipeline that cuts false positive alert volume in SOCRadar feeds for one of our customers. Alongside that, I use Claude (API + Claude Code) to design and automate the surrounding workflows — two ends of the same problem: self hosted models for data that can't leave the building, frontier models for the engineering work around them.

The throughline: AI powered security — building the systems that defend, and Understanding how those same AI systems get attacked, backed by the MAD20 MITRE ATT&CK certification track (CTI, Fundamentals, and Adversary Emulation done so far).
